A pale abstract ground, almost all warm off-white paper. One thin wash of
colour bleeds across it like watercolour soaking into wet paper: a faint dusty
indigo tint at the left edge, thinning through a muted mauve and magenta in
the middle, warming to a soft rose and fading out to bare paper at the right.
The tint is diluted and chalky, never vivid and never dark: the deepest colour
is a mid-tone, there is no black, no shadow and no dense mass anywhere. The
colour has no shape of its own, no orbs, no spots, no separate patches, only
one continuous gradient with no visible seam and no edge. Most of the frame is
bare pale paper. A fine even film grain sits over the whole frame. No subject,
no horizon, no object, no type. Flat and quiet, like a page lit from behind.
