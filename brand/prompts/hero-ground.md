A smooth digital mesh gradient, the background of a modern website hero, clean
and luminous. Large soft fields of colour sweep diagonally from the top left
toward the right and melt into one another with no edges, no shapes and no
texture in the colour itself: a clear deep indigo at the top left, a clear
magenta through the upper middle, warming into a soft rose toward the top
right, each blending into the next over a long distance with no visible seam
and no grey, muddy or dull passage anywhere in between. The colours stay clean
and saturated at a mid-tone: the indigo is deep but never black, there is no
shadow and no dense dark mass. The lower right of the frame is bare warm
off-white, about half of the frame, and the colour fades into it cleanly. A
fine, even, slightly visible film grain sits over the whole frame and is the
only texture. Flat, rendered, no subject, no horizon, no object, no type.
