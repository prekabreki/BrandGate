The leftmost slice points down, so its lower corner is its own centre line, 108.3
units of the 739 unit band in from the left edge. Every line of type on a surface
takes that x, whether the mark sits above it or beside it, so the H stem reads as
hanging from the first apex. The first corner of the second slice sits 19 units
further right; the apex is the datum.
