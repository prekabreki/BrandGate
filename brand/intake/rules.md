# Handsel, the rules the gate reads

## Mark
- The mark is six pie slices in one level band, apex down then apex up in turn, at a band aspect close to 3:1 (measured 2.95:1). The flow runs through all six slices as one continuous field.
- Clear space is one slice radius on every side of the band.
- The mark has a minimum width of 96 px on screen and 28 mm in print.
- The mark is never rotated, never mirrored, never outlined and never given a shadow. The band is always level.
- A single slice may stand alone as the avatar or favicon unit.
- Type beside or below the mark starts on the first slice's apex line, 14.65 percent of the mark's width in from the left edge. Type never starts on the band's edge.

## Colour
- Paper `#FAFAF8` is the default ground and Night `#0D0B18` is the dark ground. Mist `#ECEAF6` is the ground for flat surfaces.
- Ink `#111114` is text and the mono mark. Graphite `#4A4A55` is secondary text, readable at any size, for example graphite on mist.
- Indigo `#4B3BE8` is the one flat accent. No other colour is used as a flat accent.
- Deep `#1E1A5C`, Violet `#7A4FE0`, Magenta `#B44C9E` and Rose `#D9628A` exist only as gradient stops. Magenta is never flat.
- No gradient stop is ever used as text. Rose `#D9628A` as text on mist fails contrast.
- Text is ink, graphite, or the text gradient on display type only.

## Gradient
- There is one gradient, the flow, running left to right from Deep `#1E1A5C` at 0 percent through Indigo `#4B3BE8` at 28 percent, Violet `#7A4FE0` at 50 percent as the seam, Magenta `#B44C9E` at 74 percent, to Rose `#D9628A` at 100 percent.
- The flow is raw on the mark, at full strength with no softening.
- Display type takes the text variant of the flow, which starts from Ink `#111114` instead of Deep.
- As a ground the flow is softened to a wash, deep bleeding into indigo, violet, magenta and rose, laid on paper or night as orbs, a veil and grain.
- The flow never appears on a shape that is not a slice or type.

## Surface
- Paper `#FAFAF8` is the default surface and Night `#0D0B18` is the dark surface. Flat panels and cards sit on Mist `#ECEAF6`.
- Glass and grain appear only where there is depth. A flat surface has no glass and no grain.
- A ground wash is built from orbs, a veil and grain over paper or night, never a hard gradient fill.
- Every line of type on a surface takes the same left edge, the first apex line, whether the mark sits above it or beside it.

## Type
- Archivo is used for everything that is read. IBM Plex Mono is for labels, code and data only, never for prose.
- Display is Archivo 800 with letter spacing of -0.045 em, two lines maximum.
- The wordmark is Archivo 800 in title case, set in the text gradient or in Ink `#111114`.
- Body is Archivo 400 at 17 px with a line height of 1.5, under 75 characters a line.
- Secondary text is Graphite `#4A4A55`.

## Motif
- The six slices are the only motif. The mark is one gesture and no second graphic device is introduced.
- The single slice is the only motif that may stand alone, as the avatar or favicon unit.
- The flow appears only on a slice or on type. No other shape carries the flow.

## Motion
- The flow moves in one direction, left to right, from deep to rose.
- The band stays level in motion. The mark is never rotated or mirrored between frames.
- The brand stays itself across a hero, a story card, a Slack avatar and a printed poster. Movement never changes the slice geometry.
